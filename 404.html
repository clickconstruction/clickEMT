That page is not here

The address you opened is not a page on clickemt.com. If someone sent it to you so you could join an incident, what you need is the six-letter join code, not the link.

Part of an incident right now?

Enter the join code from your join card, from Operations' screen or from another unit's phone. It opens the field app on this phone with the code filled in; you check in with your name and your unit.

No code? It is on the join card at the check-in table and on the screen at the base. Ask whoever sent you, or the person running the base. Only the incident's Operations can add you.

Running the base?

The base lives at the front door: sign in there to open an incident or join one as Operations.
