# 3. Lt. Ana Ramirez, unit lead on foot

**Who you are.** On foot in a division with a crew of two, wet gloves, a phone in a chest pocket. You need one tap to clear an address, a photo that records which way you faced, your assignment and channel without asking, help instantly, and a head count without typing.

**Where you work.** The phone. The home screen is built around you.

**In the mock.** The phone tab is Unit 3. To be Ana, press **Menu**, then **Sign out**, and sign in as `unit1@mock.local`: **Send code**, then the code the mock shows under the form (no password). Tap Cedar Fork Flooding under "Incidents you are already in". You are Unit 1, an engine, in A-1 with Ramirez and Okafor.

## Your jobs

### 1. Mark this address clear

- **Phone** · Press **Address clear**. Type the address, pick the occupants (Unknown, Nobody home, Evacuated, Sheltering in place, Refused to leave), count the people needing help, add the water depth, damage, hazards and the marking you painted, and a note for the next crew.
- **Phone** · Press **Searched, save** at the foot of the sheet. It is below the fold on a phone; scroll once.

### 2. Send a photo that records which way you faced

- **Phone** · Press **Take photo**, then **Send**. The compass heading goes with it; the base reads "Facing E 095°". The photo is re-encoded on the phone, so no GPS data leaves with it.
- **Phone** · With no signal the photo waits in the outbox and goes when there is one.

### 3. What is my assignment and what channel am I on

- **Phone** · The **Your zone** card on the home screen: the division's code and name, the work assignment, special instructions, "Radio TAC 2 · Supervisor …" and the objective it serves.
- **Phone** · **Menu → Channels** is the communications plan (ICS 205).
- **Phone** · **Menu → Briefing** is your briefing, assembled from the incident: situation, your job, who you work with, work area, radio, supplies, shift, safety and PPE. It stays current.

### 4. Mayday

- **Phone** · Press the red **Need help now**, then **Send alert**. The screen says "Alert sent · Waiting for Operations · Your location keeps sending", then "Operations acknowledged". Press **Stand down, all OK** when it is over.

### 5. Answer the PAR without typing

- **Phone** · A head count takes over the screen: "Head count (PAR)" with the names checked in with you. Press **All 2 accounted for**.
- **Phone** · If someone is missing, press **Someone's missing**, tick who, press **Send**. Operations sees the name on its bar and the person turns red on its board; an emergency is raised for you.

### 6. Mark the division clear with the marking we painted

- **Phone** · Press **Zone clear**. It sends the whole division to Operations as primary search done or cleared, with the marking drawn for you (time out, team, hazards, people found). Press **Confirm clear**.

### Also yours

- **Phone** · **If someone is hurt** on the home screen: the medic on your unit or the nearest one, the emergency channel, the nearest hospital with directions, **Ask for the ambulance**.
- **Phone** · **Inbox → Message Operations**: say how urgent in four words (Act on now, Before nightfall, Can wait until tomorrow, Blocks further operations), use a quick chip or your own words, and **Attach my location**.
- **Phone** · **Inbox → Ask for something** sends Logistics a request: what, how many, by when, where.
- **Phone** · On a PAR, **Scan them in · 0 of 2** beside All accounted for (v55): scan each band in front of you and the card marks them scanned; the answer says how many were by scan. One not scanned is for you to say.
- **Phone** · A safety message takes over the phone until you answer Copy, Will do or Cannot.
- **Phone** · An invitation (v56): the email's link opens the field app signed in, with "You're invited · Cedar Fork Flooding · as a unit, Unit 4, by Marcus Bell"; say who you are if not yet on record, and **Check in · no code to type**.
- **Phone** · Bands (v55): at check-in, type each person's band number in **Band №** on their row and the scan is their check-in; or **Menu → Scan a band** later and pick whose it is. Scan a band in the division and the sheet preselects **Entering A-2**; Operations' board then reads "seen entering A-2 07:05" for that person, never "is at". When the camera will not open, type the band's number under it.
- **Phone** · On the house record, each person's row has **On which run** when a bus or a van is planned or loading (v54): your own division's run first. Saved, the family is on that run and on its way to its destination; the desk at the gym sees them under the bus before it arrives. If Operations makes your unit the driver of a run, your home screen shows **You are Van 4** with Loading, Leaving and Arrived as one tap each.
- **Phone** · **Menu → People on the unit**: Check out, Back in or Edit each person. **Menu → Check out** walks the demobilization list; nothing is required.

## Rough edges

- Searched, save is a scroll down on a phone.
- Zone clear asks about the whole division, not your part of it.
