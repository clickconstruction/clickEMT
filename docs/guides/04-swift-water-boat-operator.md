# 4. Rosa Alvarez, swift-water boat operator

**Who you are.** In the water at the low crossing with a mutual-aid crew. You need rotation prompts, the boat ramp and the shelter on the map, intake for the people you pull out, and a head count that knows who is in the boat.

**Where you work.** The phone.

**In the mock.** Press **Menu**, **Sign out**, and sign in as `unit2@mock.local` with any password of eight or more characters. Tap Cedar Fork Flooding under "Incidents you are already in". You are Unit 2, a boat crew from Travis County Rescue, on B-1 with Nguyen and Patel.

## Your jobs

### 1. Where is the boat ramp and the shelter

- **Phone** · **Nearby** on the home screen lists the nearest staging area, shelter, hospital and boat ramp with **Directions**. The seeded incident has no ramp yet; Operations places one on the base under Facilities, and your briefing's Work area then names it with the distance.

### 2. How long has this crew been in the water

- **Phone** · The first row of the home screen reads "Not in the water · **In the water**". One tap starts the clock: "In the water 0 min · rotation 45 min". The same row is in the menu.
- **Phone** · Past the rotation the row turns yellow: "past the rotation: swap the crew", with **Later** to put the words away and keep the clock. It asks again each rotation.
- **Phone** · Press **Out of the water** when you are out. Checking out ends the clock too. Operations sees the time on your row, orange past the rotation.

### 3. Keep track of my people

- **Phone** · **Menu → People on the unit**: who is checked in with you, each with Check out, Back in and Edit. Per-person tracking when phones are in dry bags is not built; the unit is one dot on the map.

### 4. Log who we pulled out of that house

- **Phone** · Press **Address clear**, then **Add who and where they went**. Each row is a person or a family: who, their condition, and where they went (the shelter first, the hospital, With family, Own transport, Not known).
- **Phone** · Press **Searched, save**. The shelter desk sees them under "Arriving from crews" and signs them in with one tap when they arrive; the 214 says who you sent where.

- **Phone** · Bands (v55): your crew's bands scanned (or their numbers typed at check-in) are their check-in; scanned again boarding the boat, the board reads "seen boarding Boat run 3 17:40" for each of them, so the base knows out of sight from unaccounted for. On a PAR, **Scan them in** counts the bands in front of you.
- **Phone** · When a bus or a boat run is planned to your pickup, each person's row has **On which run** (v54); saved, the family rides on it and the desk sees them under the run before it arrives. If Operations makes your unit the driver of a boat run, your home screen shows **You are Boat run 3** with Loading, Leaving and Arrived as one tap each.

### 5. Need help now

- **Phone** · Press **Need help now**, then **Send alert**. Two taps. Stand down when it is over.

### 6. What are we assigned to

- **Phone** · The **Your zone** card: "B-1 · Mill Street bridge approach", the words of the assignment, the radio channel, the objective.

### 7. Answer the head count

- **Phone** · The PAR card lists Nguyen and Patel. Press **All 2 accounted for**, or **Someone's missing**, tick the name, **Send**.

## Rough edges

- The water clock is per unit, not per person; a crew that swaps one person out keeps one clock for the boat.
- The row shows even on a dry assignment.
- The nudge is on the screen only; a phone in a dry bag makes no sound.
