# 5. Priya Natarajan, Safety Officer

**Who you are.** You walk between divisions, watching the water and the crews. You need to push a safety message that takes over every phone, to see who has not acknowledged, to read hazards as they come in, and to know who has been on scene or in the water too long.

**Where you work.** The base to send and to watch; a phone to read what the crews see.

**In the mock.** The base tab is Operations, which is where your message goes out. Your seat is the Safety Officer line on the **Period** sheet. The phone tab (Unit 3) shows you what a crew sees when your message lands.

## Your jobs

### 1. Push a safety message that takes over every phone

- **Base** · Under **Message the field**, choose everyone (or one unit, or a division's unit), tick **Safety**, write the message, send. Every phone it reaches shows it over whatever was open until the unit answers Copy, Will do or Cannot.

### 2. Who has not acknowledged

- **Base** · The yellow SAFETY bar under the header reads "waiting on Unit 3, Brush 12, …" until the last one answers, then clears. The Messages list says "All N acknowledged".

### 3. Read the hazards as they come in, and where they are

- **Base** · A hazard is a log line ("Hazard in A-2: downed power line, reported by Unit 3"), a triangle on the map where the unit stood, an orange outline on its division and a chip on the division's panel. Press **Resolved** on it when it is dealt with; the outline stays as "a hazard was reported here".
- **Phone** · A crew's briefing carries the open hazards near it.

### 4. Who has been on scene too long

- **Base** · **People** shows each person with "On scene 3 h 17 min, since 03:53"; **Hours** totals them by person and unit.
- **Base** · Press **Period** and set **Hours on scene before Safety is told** beside the rotation (12 unless you change it, 0 turns it off; it carries into the next period). Past it a person's row reads "On scene 13 h 12 min, since 03:53 · past 12 h" in orange on the People board and on the unit's panel, the board's count line says "2 past 12 h on scene", and the shift-change briefing's Needs attention, the situation report's **On scene too long** and the 208 name them, longest first.
- **Base** · A boat crew's time in the water is on its row and panel, orange past the rotation and "probably left running" past three rotations. The briefing, the sitrep and the 208 list who is in the water.

### 5. Write the period's safety message and set the rotation

- **Base** · Press **Period**. The **Safety message and notes for the period** box prints on every 204 and on the 208, and reads in every unit's briefing. The rotation in the water (minutes; 0 turns the nudge off) is on the same sheet and carries into the next period.

## Rough edges

- There is no list of open hazards of its own; the log, the map and the division panels are where they are.
- The hours-on-scene warning does not push to the phone; the crew sees it only when they open Your briefing from the menu (the Shift card says "Your crew has been on scene 13 h, past the period's 12 hours"), so Safety or Operations still tells them.
