# 6. The Documentation Unit

**Who you are.** Often one borrowed clerk. You need a 214 that writes itself, a 211 with names, the situation report at the top of each period, hours for Finance, and a replay for the after-action review.

**Where you work.** The base.

**In the mock.** The base tab, signed in as Operations, has a morning of log lines, three units with people, and one period to print.

## Your jobs

### 1. Print the 214 for the period

- **Base** · The activity log is the 214; the database writes it, line by line, from everything the field and Operations do. Press **Print 214** on the log's toolbar: the IC history, the Notable lines first, then every line oldest first.
- **Base** · Press the star on a line to make it Notable. **Pop out** puts the log in its own window for a second screen.

### 2. Print the 211 with the names

- **Base** · Press **People**, then **Print 211**: every unit with who checked it in, each person's time in and out, and "identity not given" where Operations still has to verify someone in person.

### 3. Make the situation report and get it to the EOC

- **Base** · Press **Sitrep**. Write the three boxes (Situation, Resources needed, Outlook) and press **Save the words**. The rest is assembled: who is in charge, who is on scene from where, divisions by status, people sheltered, moved to hospital and expected, open requests, cleared and checked photos, who is in the water.
- **Base** · **Copy as text** or **Print**. Getting it to the EOC is copy and paste.

### 4. Play back the day for the after-action review

- **Base** · Press **Replay**: a slider, Play, this period, the last hour or the whole incident, and a speed. The photos appear as they were taken and the log line of the moment reads under the slider.

### 5. Find who said what at 10:40

- **Base** · Type in **Find in the log**. Filter the log by where a line came from, and follow a line's link to the photo, division, structure or facility it is about.

### 6. Hours for Finance

- **Base** · **People → Hours**: every person and unit with time in, out and the hours, for a period or the whole incident, as a page or **Export CSV**. A row counted to now because nobody pressed check out is marked.

## Rough edges

- The EOC gets the sitrep by copy and paste; there is no send.
- The picker and the phone show the period as it was typed; the reports show the real times.
