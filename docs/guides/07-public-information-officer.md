# 7. The Public Information Officer

**Who you are.** Under pressure for pictures within the hour. You need only the photos Operations has cleared, with time, place and a caption, and nothing that shows a victim, a plate or an address.

**Where you work.** The base.

**In the mock.** The base tab is Operations, which can do everything the PIO does. Your seat is the Public Information Officer line on the **Period** sheet; one seeded photo is already cleared.

## Your jobs

### 1. Which photos are cleared

- **Base** · Press **Public** on the header: your own page with who is PIO, "N photos cleared for the public, M checked", then one line per cleared photo with its time, unit, division, whether it is checked and its public caption; a line opens the photo. (The same section is at the foot of Operations' home panel.)

### 2. Make sure none shows a victim or an address

- **Base** · Open a cleared photo. Write the **Public caption** (what the public reads; never the unit's note or a house number) and press **Checked: no face, no plate, no house number**. **Withdraw the check** undoes it. Both go in the 214.
- **Base** · Clearing itself is Operations' call, on the same panel (**Cleared for public information**); your check is a mark, not a second approval.

### 3. Get them as files with time, place and a caption

- **Base** · Press **Download the public set**, on the Public page or the home panel. Only checked photos leave, each named by its time and place with the caption written into the file, and a captions.txt with the time, the place to the street and the caption. Never coordinates, never the unit's note. Cleared photos not yet checked are listed as missing.

### 4. Am I named as PIO

- **Base** · The **Period** sheet seats you; the 203, the 207 and the briefing name you, and the sitrep's last section says how many photos are cleared and checked, to you.

### 5. Who is in charge, and who is on scene from where

- **Base** · The header names the IC and the lead agency; the sitrep and the **Agencies** section list every agency on scene; the unified command is on the Period sheet.

## Rough edges

- A photo outside any division is placed at the base in the captions.
- Phone photos are re-encoded before upload, so they carry no GPS data; the check is for what is in the frame.
