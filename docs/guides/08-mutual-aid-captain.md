# 8. Capt. Reyes, mutual-aid captain from two counties over

**Who you are.** You arrive at staging knowing nobody and nothing about the town. You need to join in a minute, prove who you are, put your crew in, get a 204 on paper with a map, find the hospital and the channels, and be released cleanly at the end.

**Where you work.** The phone, and the base for the printed 204 and the join card someone hands you.

**In the mock.** On the phone tab press **Menu**, **Sign out**, then **Create account** with any new email and a password of eight or more characters. Check in with the code `CEDAR7`.

## Your jobs

### 1. Check in at staging

- **Phone** · **Scan a code** from the card, Operations' screen or another unit's phone, or type it. Give your unit its own callsign, your agency, what the unit is (engine, boat crew, swift-water team, high-water vehicle, ambulance, search, K9, drone, pump, shelter desk), and **How many with you, including you**.
- **Phone** · Press **Check in**. The base sees you at once, with your kind, your agency and your people.

### 2. Show them who you are

- **Phone** · **Identify yourself** opens before your first check-in: your name and phone, and **Scan the back of your licence** or **Photo of the front** ("No licence to hand? Type its number"). A name and a phone alone check in too; the base then marks you "identity not given" and Operations verifies you in person.
- **Phone** · Another unit never sees your licence. Thirty days after the incident closes, the licence record clears by rule.

### 3. Put my crew in

- **Phone** · **Add a person** at check-in: name, rank or role, qualifications (swift water, boat, rope, EMT, paramedic, K9, hazmat, drone), buddy. Later, **Menu → People on the unit** to edit, check someone out or back in.
- **Phone** · **Menu → Code** shows the join code as a QR so your second crew can join from your phone.

### 4. My 204 on paper, and what channel we are on

- **Base** · Open your division and press **Print the 204**: the assignment, special instructions, supervisor, channel, your names, the medical lines, and a map of the division with the structures, hazards and facilities.
- **Phone** · The **Your zone** card has the same words and the channel; **Menu → Channels** is the whole plan.
- **Phone** · Your briefing opens after check-in (and stays in **Menu → Briefing**): situation, your job, who you work with, work area, radio, supplies, shift, safety and PPE, with the rotation in the water if you are a water crew. **Read later** or **Briefed, go to the map**.

### 5. Where is the hospital, where is staging

- **Phone** · **Nearby** lists staging, the shelter, the hospital and the boat ramp with **Directions**. **If someone is hurt** names the medic, the emergency channel and the nearest hospital, with **Ask for the ambulance**.

### 6. Get released

- **Phone** · Operations' release message says where to go and what to return. Answer **Will do**, then **Menu → Check out**: the demobilization list (nothing required; what is not ticked goes to Operations), a note for the next crew, **Check out**. Your water clock, if any, stops.
