# 9. Dep. Luis Moreno, sheriff's deputy at a closed bridge

**Who you are.** You work alone at a closed bridge for hours. You need to be counted without a buddy, to report a car in the water with its spot in two taps, to say the road is closed, and to be told when the post can stand down.

**Where you work.** The phone.

**In the mock.** Press **Menu**, **Sign out**, **Create account** with a new email. Check in with `CEDAR7`, your callsign, kind Other, and **How many with you, including you** set to 1.

## Your jobs

### 1. Check in so the CP knows I am at the bridge, by myself

- **Phone** · Check in with your name and phone (a licence is not required) and a count of 1. The base's People board shows "No buddy" on your unit.

### 2. Car in the water, right here

- **Phone** · Press **Report hazard**. It pins where you stand. Pick the kind (Other, for a car in the water), say what you see, **Send hazard**. It is on the base's map and in the log at once.
- **Phone** · To say how urgent it is, use **Inbox → Message Operations** instead: **Act on now**, your words, **Attach my location**, **Send**. The base shows the badge and a bubble on the map.
- **Phone** · **Need help now** is for you; use it if you are the one in trouble.

### 3. Answer the head count

- **Phone** · The PAR card takes over the screen. With no names on record it shows your count with − and +; press **All accounted for**. With your count typed at check-in it starts there.

### 4. Report the road is closed

- **Phone** · **Inbox → Message Operations**, the quick chip **Road blocked**, an urgency word, **Send**.

### 5. When can I stand down

- **Phone** · Operations' message reads "You are released. Return to staging, return the radio, check out on your phone." Press **Will do**, then **Menu → Check out**.

## Rough edges

- A hazard carries no urgency word of its own; a message does.
- The briefing's Work area says "wait at staging" for a one-person post at a bridge.
