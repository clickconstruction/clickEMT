# 10. The shelter desk volunteer

**Who you are.** At the gym door with a clipboard and a line of wet families. You need to know who is arriving on which bus, who needs oxygen or insulin, who has pets, and an answer for the woman asking after her mother.

**Where you work.** The phone someone hands you at the door; the base for whoever has a laptop at the gym.

**In the mock.** Press **Menu**, **Sign out**, **Create account** with a new email. Check in with `CEDAR7`, a unit name like "Gym desk", agency Red Cross, and the kind **Shelter desk**. Your name and phone are enough; the base marks you "identity not given" for Operations to check in person. Your shelter is the nearest open one, High school gym.

## Your jobs

Your home screen is **People here**: the shelter's count against its beds, the needs counted (oxygen, insulin, wheelchair, infant, pets), what Operations expects next, who is on their way from crews, and who is here now, newest first.

### 1. Who is coming on the next bus

- **Phone** · **Arriving** is by run (v54): each bus, van or ambulance coming to your shelter as a card, "Bus 2 from Pecan Park pickup · En route · 4 people in 1 record · left 17:05 · expected 17:30 · Travis ISD, 512 555 0177", with the families on it listed. A run that has not arrived by its time says **late**. The header says how many runs are coming; Operations' typed expected line gave way to them. Families a crew sent on no run still list under **Arriving from crews**, with the house they left and the crew that sent them.

### 2. Sign in a family

- **Phone** · Press **Sign in a person**: who (the only thing needed), how many, the needs chips, where from, where now (here first), a note. **Sign in here**.
- **Phone** · When the bus pulls in, press **They're here, all 12** on its card: every family on it is signed in at once and the run is marked arrived for Operations. Or press **Here** on one family's row as they come off, so nobody is signed in twice; add the name if you have it.

### 3. This man needs oxygen or insulin

- **Phone** · Tap the person's row, press the chip, **Save**. The header's counts follow.

### 4. The dog, the cat

- **Phone** · The **Pets** chip, and the note for what kind.

### 5. A woman is asking after her mother

- **Phone** · Type the name in **Find a person, any shelter or hospital**. It answers where anyone is now and since when, across every shelter and hospital, and lists everyone from a street.
- **Phone** · Nobody matches? **Ask Operations to look** sends "Looking for …" with your desk's phone number, and Operations answers in your inbox.

### 6. Tell them we are nearly full

- **Phone** · **Inbox → Message Operations**, an urgency word, your words, **Send**. For cots and blankets, **Inbox → Ask for something** goes to Logistics.

### Moving someone on

- **Phone** · Tap the row and change **Where now**: a hospital, another shelter, With family, Own transport. A record is moved, never removed, and each move is a line in the log.

### On the base

- **Base** · The shelter's card has its beds, the **Expected** line to type, the live count on their way, the needs, People here with Where now on each row, **Sign in a person** and **Print the list**. **Find a person** is in the header. Names never appear in anything public, and they clear by rule after the incident closes.

## Rough edges

- The quick chips in Message Operations are a search crew's (Need more hands, Water rising here, Road blocked).
- The briefing reads like a search crew's in places (staging, the map).
- A toast can sit over a sheet's buttons for a few seconds.
