# 12. The resident who refused to leave

**Who you are.** Not a user, but the person the app exists for. You need to be revisited at a set time, to have your oxygen noted, and for the next crew to know the house has been searched.

**What the app keeps for you.**

- **Phone** · The crew's house record (**Address clear**): occupants, with **Refused to leave** as one of the answers; the people needing help; the water depth and damage; the hazards; the marking painted on the house; and **Notes for the next crew** ("on oxygen, revisit at 16:00").
- **Base** · The record is on the map and in the log by address, so Operations answers "has that house been searched" from the base, and the next crew's 204 map shows it.
- **Phone** · If a crew moves you, **Add who and where they went** carries your name, condition and destination to the shelter desk, who signs you in with one tap, and **Find a person** answers your family's question at any shelter or hospital.
- Your name is seen by the incident's members only, never in anything public, and it clears by rule after the incident closes.

## Rough edges

- The revisit is a note, not a reminder that fires.
