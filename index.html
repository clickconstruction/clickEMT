Part of an incident right now?

Enter the six-letter join code from your join card, from Operations' screen or from another unit's phone. It opens the field app on this phone with the code filled in; you check in with your name and your unit.

No code? It is on the join card at the check-in table and on the screen at the base. Ask whoever sent you, or the person running the base. Only the incident's Operations can add you.

One picture of the incident, from the boat to the command post.

A map at the base and an app on every phone, sharing one picture: where the units are, what they have cleared, who needs help, and the ICS forms written as it happens.

  • Field first. One tap in the rain with gloves on, and a dead spot never loses a report.
  • The paperwork writes itself. The 214, the 211, every 204, the 209 and the IAP come from what the crews send.
  • Nothing is deleted, nothing is public by default. The code is open on GitHub.

Running the base?

Sign in with the account your agency gave you to open an incident, join one as Operations, and print the join card.

Thinking of piloting it?

A pilot runs on your own incidents or a season: a briefing for the command staff and the crews, support during an incident, and an evaluation at the end. Nothing a crew sends is deleted, and personal records clear by rule after the close.
