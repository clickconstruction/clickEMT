One picture of the incident, from the boat to the command post.

A map at the base and an app on every phone, sharing one picture: where the units are, what they have cleared, who needs help, and the ICS forms written as it happens.

  • Field first. One tap in the rain with gloves on, and a dead spot never loses a report.
  • The paperwork writes itself. The 214, the 211, every 204, the 209 and the IAP come from what the crews send.
  • Nothing is deleted, nothing is public by default. The code is open on GitHub.
