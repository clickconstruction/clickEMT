a · Rectangleb · Four corners
FitsA north-up picture: a drone mosaic, a satellite crop, a county orthophoto, a scanned plan.Any picture of flat ground: a photo out of an aircraft window, a mosaic flown on a heading, a plan photographed on a table.

Build first

b · Four corners. The pictures the app names, Civil Air Patrol photos, are shot from a window and never fit a rectangle; a day on rectangle handles would be thrown away. The card and the handles are the same in both. What (b) adds is one column, a migration and about eighty lines both views share, and bounds still holds the corners' box.

If the pictures you expect are north-up exports (a drone mosaic, a satellite crop), (a) is enough and a day.

Decided here, and yours to decide

Decided here (say if any is wrong)
  • Choose the file and it is on the map. No coordinates typed, ever.
  • It lands over the view at half strength, 70 % of the width, its own shape.
  • The map still pans and zooms while placing. The handles are markers, so they follow.
  • Drag the picture to move it, a corner to stretch or narrow it. In (b) a round handle above it turns it, shape kept; in (a) the edges move one edge each.
  • Name and Taken at on the card, filled from the file (its name; its date, the camera's when it has one), both changeable. The slider's value is what is saved; it starts at 50 so the roads show through.
  • Save writes the row as today (data.addOverlay); the trigger writes the line. Adjust on a row reopens the handles and logs “updated”.
  • Undo steps back one drag (⌘Z). Cancel takes the picture off; Escape is Cancel. Arrows nudge a pixel, Shift ten.
  • Under the divisions, as today, so their outlines stay readable over it.
  • The base places; the phone shows.
Yours to decide
  • Which first: (b) as above, or (a) if the pictures are north-up.
  • A picture with no GPS in it lands over the view either way. A JPEG with a GPS point could land centred on it (an EXIF read, about forty lines, no library). Worth it now?
  • A second picture over the first: newest on top. While placing, dim the others, hide them, or leave them?
  • The phone too? A drone crew with a tablet could place in the field: thumb-sized handles and the outbox. Later, or never.
  • Turning: a handle is drawn. A slider on the card instead?
  • The paper: should the 204 map carry the picture once one is on the map? A rectangle can; a warp cannot on SVG.
