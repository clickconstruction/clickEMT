People and tags

A wristband with a QR code per person, scanned at check-in, at the division, at a transport and at the shelter door. A scan is a "seen" record; the board shows last seen per person; the PAR can be answered by scanning. Made-up data; press the buttons.

Print bands · Cedar Fork Flooding

A sheet of bands: the incident's name, a number and the code. No name on a band; a lost one tells nobody anything. Boxes of bands can be printed before the season and assigned by scanning.

The band sheet, from the base. Tyvek wristbands on a sheet printer with a template, a few cents each. QR, not NFC, because every phone's camera reads it.
