Show me around

The first time the base opens on a device, a label sits over each part of the screen: what it is called, what it is for, and what to do there first. Click a label and it goes; use the part underneath and it goes too. Clear all, or Escape, puts the rest away, and Settings brings them back. Nothing is written anywhere but this device. The backdrop is the base at v57 as the mock seeds it, at 2000 px wide, dark; press the labels. Built as v58 (docs/punchlist.md, section 17).

Decided here, and yours to decide

Decided here (say if any is wrong)
  • Every label at once, not a tour. A tour says one thing at a time; the labels show the shape of the whole screen, numbered in the order a new Operations would use it, and you clear the ones you already know first.
  • One sentence of purpose and one of what to do first. Never a manual; the guides in docs/guides are the manual.
  • Using the part clears its label. Click the label, or click through it to the part: the first click clears, the next one works. Clear all and Escape clear the rest.
  • Per device, like the theme. Shown on the first open of the base on this device (ic.base.shown in localStorage), never again until Settings → Show me around. Nothing goes to the database; the mock needs nothing.
  • The alarm line is never covered while something is open on it, and an emergency arriving clears every label at once: the base rings, the bar is red, the labels are gone.
  • The base only, for now. The phone already shows Your briefing once per check-in and its home screen is built around one person.
Yours to decide
  • The words on the switch. "Show me around" is on the mock-up; "Discovery mode" is the name in the plan. One of them goes on Settings.
  • Ten labels or fewer. The legend (6) and the Operations code (9) could fold into the map (5) and the join code (8), leaving eight.
  • On the public demo too? /try/ frames the base for visitors with its own tours. The labels could show there on the first open as well, before the tours.
  • Should a new incident start even emptier? A fresh incident has no divisions, units or log lines, so its screen is already quieter than this seeded one; the labels could then also say "nothing here yet: draw the boundary".
  • The header grouped (the finding above): with the labels, after them, or not at all.

Decided here, and yours to decide

Decided here (say if any is wrong)
  • Twelve words a label, titles in the screen's own words, the number on the part too.
  • The division at the right of a unit's row, the small facts under it, groups as headings.
  • The Operations code is dots until Show; the rest of the codes' card opens on the pill, not on hover.
  • The live card is on hover and on click (a touch screen has no hover), closed by a click outside or Escape.
  • Boundary first is a nudge, not a rule: Division stays pressable with no boundary, as today.
  • The gear holds four things: the incident's settings, this device (Light, Show me around), Switch incident, Sign out. Light leaves the header row.
  • The head count by Find a person reads the same numbers as the Units heading; the orange part is the hours-on-scene threshold, already on the board.
  • "In it now" counts phones inside the polygon and bands last seen entering it; a phone that has not reported for five minutes is not counted, and the card says so.
Yours to decide
  • The 211's table (10) is the page and the print only; the text and the email keep the lines, since a text table does not survive a phone's mail. Decided so, and built as v71 and v72.
  • How to cut the releases. Small and together as v60: labels (1), the codes behind the pill (3), the live card (4), the drawing buttons (5), Layers flipped (6), the gear (7), the head count (8), the division's hover card (9). On its own: the units section (2), which touches the panel every suite reads. The People board, Replay and the pages as modals follow their own mock-ups.
  • A quiet phone: the live card marks a unit quiet after how long? Five minutes is drawn.
  • Where the codes' card closes: a click outside, Escape, or only the pill again.
  • Hours and the People board at the foot of Units as links, or kept as buttons in the heading.
