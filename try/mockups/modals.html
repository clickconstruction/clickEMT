Under 900 px: full screen, always. The base's narrow layout stacks the map and the panel; a window with margins would waste the screen. Expand is hidden and the setting is ignored. The alarm line still comes first.

Decided here, and yours to decide

Recommended

Every page opens as a window under the alarm line, as wide as the page needs, over the dimmed map. Expand makes it full screen and the base remembers that on this device. The alarm line is never covered, in either state. Printing, the pop-out and the papers' words do not change. One rule for all seven pages and the printed forms.

Decided here (say if any is wrong)
  • All seven move together, and the 204, 211, 214, join card and band sheet with them. One setting, ic.base.pages, per device like the theme. The papers do not stay full screen: an 820 px sheet in a window looks like paper on the desk, and the boards lose nothing at 1280 (the People board stops at 1100 px today).
  • The window is as wide as the page needs: 870 px for a paper, 1150 px for a board, never wider than the screen less 24 px a side, 20 px above and below. On a wide screen the map shows around it.
  • The alarm line is never covered. As a window, the page sits under the header and the alarm line, which stay live. Full screen folds the header away and keeps the alarm line as the top strip. An emergency turns it red in front of whatever is open, and the base rings as today. Press the red chip above to see both states.
  • Printing is unchanged. The window is the same #briefing; print CSS already makes it static and hides everything else. The dim and the window's edges go with two lines in @media print.
  • Escape closes, and so does a click on the dim, as the division's card does. A form in the page with typed words is the exception: then Escape leaves the field and the click does nothing until Save or Cancel.
  • Under 900 px, full screen always; Expand is hidden and the setting ignored.
  • The pop-out log is unchanged. It never opens a page itself; a line's link asks the main window, which opens the page in the remembered state.
  • Expand sits beside Close, the two window controls together at the bar's right end; the page's own buttons stay before them.
Yours to decide
  • The words. Expand / Shrink is drawn. Full screen / Window says the state instead of the move; ⤢ alone is shorter but a new sign to learn.
  • Whether the header stays in full screen. Drawn folded away, as it is covered today. Kept, full screen gains only the dim and 40 px of height over the window.
  • A Pop out for the papers. The Briefing or the IAP alone in a window of its own, like the log, for the second screen at the command post. Not drawn; a natural next step once the pages are windows.
  • How dark the dim is. 38% black is drawn; the division's card uses 45%.
