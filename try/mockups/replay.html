Decided here, and yours to decide

Decided here (say if any is wrong)
  • Each range says where its positions begin ("This period · from 08:00", "Whole incident · from 06:40"); Last hour stays bare. The slider still spans the whole range, so the log before the first phone is reachable, with the run-up hatched and dated.
  • Out of range in two places: "no positions yet" beside the greyed time, and the line under says "Positions start 06:40" before the log line. The bar stays two rows.
  • Pick a time… is an option of the range menu, not a button, so the bar keeps one row of controls; the sheet opens under it, inside the bar, with a date and a time as two boxes.
  • A picked moment outside the range widens it to the whole incident and lands the slider there; inside, the range stays. The menu goes back to naming the range.
  • Slow / Normal / Fast with what a second of play shows; the × goes into the hover title.
For the build
  • The first position comes from the database: the earliest ic_tracks row of the incident, read once when the bar opens (the members' select policy on ic_tracks already allows it, so one ordered select at … limit 1 in data.js, or a small ic_replay_first function). With no row, the incident's opening stands in and the bar shows the no-positions state. The mock mirrors it from its own ic_tracks.
  • Never the future. The pick is bounded by now, not by the period's planned end (19:00 here): a moment past now is refused as "has not happened yet". replayRange already stops an open period at now.
  • The slider gets a custom track (the hatch cannot sit on a native one); the thumb and track read --accent and --control-border, the hatch --muted over --tint-blue, so dark mode needs nothing extra.
Yours to decide
  • Hatched, dimmed or cut off (the chips over the live bar). Hatched is drawn: it keeps the pre-arrival log reachable and looks like what it is. Cut off is simplest and can never be clicked into, at the cost of those minutes.
  • Pick a time… in the menu, or click the time. Clicking 09:14 to type a moment is one step shorter and keeps the menu to three ranges, but is not discoverable; the menu option is drawn.
  • The wording of the speeds: A is drawn, B and C are on the page.
  • Past periods in the menu ("Period 1 · 06:12 to 08:00") once an incident has several: not drawn.
