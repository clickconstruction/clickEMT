A transport on the map

Todd, 18 October, of the transport board at v54: For transports we should make it easy to expose a transport on the map view. A run was a row on the board and a line on the shelter card; the map, which is what Operations watches, showed nothing of it. This page is the record of what was built, drawn after the build: every open run on the base's map, the bus where the driver's phone is. Built as v70 (docs/punchlist.md, section 29; the re-walk in docs/walkthroughs.md). Made-up data; nothing here moves.

What it is made of no migration; a small release on the base alone

  • No new column. The pickup is the facility's spot or the division's centroid, the destination its facility, the vehicle the driver's last ic_positions row: runGeometry in rules.js reads them as they are, so nothing changed in the database or on the phone.
  • Five minutes. The vehicle shows while the driver's position is fresher than RUN_FRESH_MS, the division hover card's rule for "in it now"; older than that, the pill goes back to the route.
  • One layer. renderRuns in js/base/map.js redraws the runs layer on every change like the photos and hazards; setReplay clears it.
  • The words are the board's. The pill is transportLabel, the hover runLine, the state transportLate; the mock's Bus 2 is driven by Unit 2, so its phone is the bus, and the transports smoke suite moves it.

Decided, and still open

Decided in the build (say if any is wrong)
  • Where is the bus? On the driver's phone while its position is under five minutes old; else nowhere, and the pill sits on the route, dashed, which says "no fresh position" without pretending. chosen The other way, the pill always on the route, loses the one thing Todd asked to see.
  • A pickup typed in words? No dot; the line runs from the vehicle to the destination, and with no vehicle either the pill sits on the destination. chosen
  • Which runs? Every open run: planned, loading, en route and arrived, so a planned run is findable on the map before the bus exists; done and withdrawn come off. chosen
  • Where does a click go? To the board, open on that run; the board stays the place to press buttons. chosen
  • Show on map on the board's rows and the home panel's runs, closing the board since it covers the map. chosen
  • The colours. Planned grey, loading purple, en route blue, arrived green, late orange. chosen Loading in the board's orange tint would read as late on the map.
  • Replay takes the runs off with the live units. chosen
Yours to decide (what to do next, recommended first)
  • A Runs box in the Layers panel's overlays column? Not built: the runs are few and come off when done. open Recommended: wait for a walk on a busy day; it would be a per-device box like the overlays' show box, one line in the panel and one in renderRuns.
  • A pickup clicked on the map (punchlist section 15, from v54), so a run has a point of its own and the dot and the line start where the bus loads. open Recommended: next, when the crews ask; it is a column and a map mode.
  • The run on the phone's mini map, so the driver sees the line to the destination. open Recommended: not yet; the driver has the You are Bus 2 card and the satnav link.
  • A trail behind the bus. open Recommended: no; the unit's breadcrumbs already show.
